# Nordic Soft Cards

## Organizing idea

Modular cards such as Important now, Messages, Next and Home status.

## Best suited for

Flexible dashboards and gradual feature growth.

## Surface behavior

- **iPhone:** personal priority and actions first
- **Shared display:** household-safe group view readable at a distance
- **Shelly Wall Display XL:** one or a few location-relevant items with large controls

## Guardrail

Limit the number of modules and provide curated defaults.
