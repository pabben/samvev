# Action Board

## Organizing idea

Content is grouped as New, Planned, Needs action and Done.

## Best suited for

Turning AI findings and household messages into transparent follow-up.

## Surface behavior

- **iPhone:** personal priority and actions first
- **Shared display:** household-safe group view readable at a distance
- **Shelly Wall Display XL:** one or a few location-relevant items with large controls

## Guardrail

Keep ordinary messages separate from tasks so everything does not feel like work.
