# ADR: Support claimable tasks and individual/shared rewards

- **Status:** Accepted product direction
- **Date:** 2026-09-06

## Context

Children should be able to choose available household tasks and contribute toward personal or shared rewards.

## Decision

Add assigned, claimable and collaborative tasks; optional approval; individual rewards; shared goals; and an auditable reward ledger.

## Consequences

The design must avoid default public rankings and manipulative reward mechanics. AI may suggest but not autonomously allocate rewards.
