# Brand foundation

## Name

**Samvev**

The name suggests separate threads woven into a coherent whole: people, information, tasks, devices and AI services.

The name remains subject to formal domain and trademark clearance.

## Taglines

Norwegian:

> Hverdagen, vevd sammen.

English:

> Everyday life, woven together.

## Brand qualities

- calm, not clinical
- capable, not complicated
- warm, not childish
- trustworthy, not magical
- modern, not trend-dependent

## Visual direction

A future mark may use interwoven lines forming an `S`, a home shape or an open loop. It must remain recognizable as a small app icon and work in one color.

## Naming system

- Samvev — product
- Samvev Display — shared-display client
- Samvev Connect — integration/API family, provisional
- Samvev Cloud — possible managed hosting, provisional

Avoid naming every feature as a separate sub-brand.
