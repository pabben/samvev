# Samvev community code of conduct

## Our standard

Participation in Samvev should be welcoming, constructive and safe. Contributors are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code rather than attacking people
- give specific, actionable feedback
- respect different experience levels, languages, family structures and accessibility needs
- protect private household and child-related information
- credit other people's work and follow its license
- disclose conflicts of interest where relevant
- accept a maintainer decision after concerns have been heard, while retaining the right to document a respectful technical disagreement

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publication of private data, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, deceptive attribution and repeated bad-faith engagement.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers, review channels, events and public interactions where a participant represents the project.

## Enforcement

Reports should be sent privately to the project maintainer through a contact method published in the GitHub profile until a dedicated address is established. Do not publish sensitive reports in an issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation or permanently remove a participant. Decisions should be proportionate, documented privately and avoid exposing reporters.

## Project responsibility

Because Samvev may process information about children and homes, careless publication of real user data is treated as a serious violation even when no malicious intent is shown.
