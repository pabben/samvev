# Name and identity policy

`Samvev` is the project's current working product name. This repository does not claim that the name or any future logo is registered as a trademark.

The open-source licenses grant rights to the code and documentation; they do not grant a right to misrepresent an unofficial build, fork or hosting service as maintained, endorsed or operated by the official project.

Reasonable uses include:

- “Based on Samvev”
- “Compatible with Samvev”
- factual references in articles, integrations and reviews

Uses that require explicit permission include:

- presenting a third-party service as “official Samvev hosting”
- copying a future logo in a way that creates likely confusion about origin
- naming a materially incompatible fork so that users are likely to mistake it for the official project

A fuller community trademark policy should be reviewed before a public hosted service or branded release launches.
