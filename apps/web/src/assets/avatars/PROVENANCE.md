Six original fictional portraits were generated specifically for Samvev on
2026-10-04 UTC with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool. They do not depict
real household members or document anyone’s age, gender or appearance. Selection
is explicit in authorized person administration; no portrait is inferred from a
name, birthday or role. Initials remain the nullable fallback.

The project distributes this original artwork under CC-BY-SA-4.0, with

character or externally fetched runtime image was used. This notice does not
claim that AI-generated material has copyright protection in every jurisdiction.

## Art direction

Warm editorial cartoon bust portraits, softly dimensional painted shading,
rounded silhouettes, expressive natural eyes, restrained friendly smile,
front-facing camera and soft upper-left daylight. Transparent square canvas,
complete hair silhouette, simple muted clothing; no text, logo, occupation
props, scenery, baked circle, halo or shadow. The first portrait established the
style reference for five separate generations. Descriptive variants are short
curls, copper bob, round glasses, side-swept fringe, silver waves and curly bun.
These describe selectable artwork, not the person choosing it.

## Optimization and provenance

Each original was a 1254 × 1254 transparent PNG. Bundled resources are
512 × 512 WebP, browser Canvas high-quality downsampling and quality 0.90.
Only size/encoding changed; there was no facial/content retouching. Original
outputs remain in the tool-owned generation directory and ignored local working
storage. The application bundles and serves these files locally, with no runtime
image generation or external image request.
