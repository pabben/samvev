# Calendar provider marks

These unmodified third-party marks identify the service selected by the user. Keep them beside the existing readable provider names; they are decorative images and do not imply endorsement or ownership by Samvev. The files are bundled locally so the interface does not request logo assets from a provider at runtime.

## Use and rights notes

- The Google mark is used only as a functional calendar-provider identifier in the connection picker and related setup views. Google's [product co-branding guidance](https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google/use-cases/product-co-branding/) says product icons should be used only when needed and in clear context. Its [general branding guidance](https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google/branding-guidelines/how-to-show-googles-brand/) says product-icon users should obtain assets through the Partner Marketing Hub and request permission. This repository does not claim that such permission has been obtained, nor a general license or Google approval.
- The Outlook icon is the current icon referenced by Outlook's own PWA manifest and is used to identify the Outlook / Microsoft 365 connection. See Microsoft's [trademark guidance](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/intellectualproperty/trademarks). Microsoft's [Cloud Storage Partner Program branding guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/cloud-storage-partner-program/online/branding) applies to that partner program and is not treated here as general permission or as evidence Samvev is a program partner. Availability from Microsoft's CDN is not treated as an open license.
- The Spond artwork came from Spond's official logo download. Spond's [recommendation page](https://www.spond.com/de/spond-empfehlen/) invites use of the mark to identify Spond on a club website and elsewhere. The ZIP contained no README or license terms, so no broader license is asserted. The complete positive mark is used on light surfaces and the complete negative mark on dark surfaces; neither is cropped, recolored, or altered.
- The Home Assistant mark remains unmodified and identifies the Home Assistant bridge used for Spond calendar sources. See the Open Home Foundation's [Home Assistant name and logo guidance](https://contributors.home-assistant.io/brand/name-and-logo/). Its trademark guidance describes non-commercial use; this project is currently distributed as free, open-source software and is not represented here as a product for sale. No open license or Foundation endorsement is claimed.

If the product's distribution or provider use changes, re-check the current official brand guidance. Keep visible provider names adjacent to marks, preserve the original artwork, and do not present these services as Samvev-owned or as endorsing Samvev.
